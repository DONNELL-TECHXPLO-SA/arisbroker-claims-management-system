## Document Requirements by Product

The lists below set out the documents typically required to substantiate a claim for each product, as
supplied by the Client. Items are not individually flagged mandatory/conditional in the BRD source at this
level of detail (unlike the generic matrix in `data-requirements.md`) — per FR-03, the checklist generated
from these lists is **advisory in all cases**: no document blocks claim registration or progression.

### 1. Property Damage (Assets)

Completed claim form · Incident report · Detailed description of what happened · Photos/videos of damage ·
SAPS case number or report · Asset register (snippet) · Proof of ownership/purchase invoices · Quotations
for repair/replacement · Valuation reports where applicable · Proof of value · Security/CCTV reports for
theft · Previous inspection/valuation reports · Proof of payment for repairs/replacement

### 2. Motor Vehicle/Fleet

Completed motor claim form · Accident/incident report · Driver's statement · Police case number/accident
report where applicable · Driver's licence · Copy of vehicle registration/licence document · Photos of
accident and damage · Vehicle tracking report · Repair quotation/assessment · Vehicle finance/lease details
where applicable · Third-party details if any · Third-party statement and claim/demand · Dashcam/CCTV
footage · Breathalyser/alcohol test where applicable · Recovery report for theft/hijacking · Keys/spare keys
· Proof of purchase/value where applicable

### 3. Public Liability

Completed liability claim form · Incident report · Detailed circumstances of incident · Third-party
claim/letter of demand · Third-party statement · Photos/videos of accident scene and damage · Witness
statements/contact details · CCTV footage · Police/SAPS report where applicable · Medical reports/invoices
for bodily injury · Repair/replacement quotations and invoices · Contracts/agreements with third party ·
Indemnity/waiver documents · Correspondence with third party · Legal summons or court documents · Internal
investigation report · Relevant risk assessments/SOPs

### 4. Commercial Crime

Completed crime claim form · Detailed incident/fraud report · Internal investigation report · Police/SAPS
case number · Affidavit/statements from relevant employees · Employee records · Disciplinary records where
applicable · Financial records/general ledger · Bank statements · Bank reconciliation · Transaction reports
· Audit and Forensic reports · Proof of misappropriated funds/assets · Emails/correspondence supporting the
loss · Access/system logs · CCTV footage · Employment contracts/job descriptions

### 5. Cyber Insurance

Cyber incident/claim notification · Detailed incident timeline · IT incident report · Forensic
investigation report · IT/security logs · Firewall/server/system logs · Evidence of unauthorised access ·
Malware/ransomware information where applicable · Correspondence with attackers, if relevant and permitted
· Data breach assessment · Details of affected data/records · Notification to regulators/affected parties
where required · Costs incurred for IT specialists · Legal costs · Data restoration costs · Business
interruption calculations · Evidence of extortion/payment demands where applicable · Vendor/third-party
contracts

### 6. Fidelity Guarantee

Completed claim form · Employee dishonesty/fidelity incident report · Detailed statement of loss ·
Police/SAPS case number · Employee statement · Employment contract/job description · Disciplinary
documentation · Forensic/audit report · Financial statements · General ledger · Bank statements/
reconciliations · Transaction records · Proof of misappropriation · CCTV/access records · Evidence of
recovery efforts · Details of employee and employment period

### 7. Professional Indemnity

Claim form · Letter of demand from third party · Details of alleged professional error/negligence · Client
correspondence · Contract/letter of appointment · Scope of services · Professional reports/work product ·
Relevant file/documentation · Expert reports where applicable · Proof of financial loss · Legal
summons/pleadings · Court documents · Witness statements · Details of previous correspondence relating to
the dispute · Fee/invoice records

### 8. Directors & Officers Liability
