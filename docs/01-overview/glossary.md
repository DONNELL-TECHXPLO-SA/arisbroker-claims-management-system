# Glossary of Terms

> Source: TechXplo – Claims Management Portal BRD v0.7 (17 Sep 2026), Appendix A.

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **AOL (Agreement of Loss)** | A document issued by the Insurer (via the Broker) for the insured Client to sign, confirming acceptance of the settlement amount offered for a claim. |
| **Assessor** | An independent party appointed by the Insurer to assess the merits and value of a claim and produce an assessment report. |
| **Broker** | A licensed intermediary (the Client in this engagement, Aris Brokers) acting between the insured (SOE) and the Insurer. |
| **ERD (Entity Relationship Diagram)** | A diagram showing the core data entities in a system and how they relate to one another; used here at a logical/conceptual level. |
| **Excess** | The portion of a loss the insured must bear before the Insurer's liability arises. |
| **FSCA** | Financial Sector Conduct Authority — the South African market conduct regulator for financial institutions, including insurance brokers. |
| **FSP** | Financial Services Provider — a licence category regulated by the FSCA under which brokers operate. |
| **Lucid** | The Client's existing document repository, described during the requirements session as functioning similarly to Microsoft OneDrive. |
| **Loss Ratio** | The ratio of claims paid to premiums paid over a given period, expressed as a percentage. |
| **MFA** | Multi-Factor Authentication — a login security control requiring more than one method of verification. |
| **POPIA** | Protection of Personal Information Act — South African legislation governing the processing of personal information. |
| **RAID** | Risks, Assumptions, Issues, and Dependencies — a standard project-management log format used to track and mitigate delivery risk. |
| **Repudiation** | The Insurer's rejection of a claim, typically because the loss is not covered under the policy. Recorded as the Repudiated outcome, with a mandatory Repudiation Reason captured. A loss that falls within or below the policy excess is recorded separately as **Within Excess**, not as a repudiation. |
| **CAR** | Construction All Risks — a commercial insurance product covering loss or damage during construction projects. |
| **CCMA** | Commission for Conciliation, Mediation and Arbitration — the South African statutory body resolving labour disputes, referenced in Employment Practices Liability claims. |
| **EAR** | Erection All Risks — a commercial insurance product covering loss or damage during the erection/installation of plant and equipment. |
| **EPL** | Employment Practices Liability — a commercial insurance product covering claims arising from employment-related disputes (e.g. unfair dismissal, discrimination). |
| **POD** | Proof of Delivery — a document confirming goods were received, commonly required for Goods in Transit and Road Carriers' Liability claims. |
| **SAPS** | South African Police Service — referenced where a police case number or report substantiates a claim. |
| **SOE** | State-Owned Enterprise — the category of client the Claims Management Portal will service in Phase 1. |
| **Within Excess** | A claim outcome where the loss amount falls below (or within) the policy excess, such that the Insurer is not liable and the full cost is borne by the insured. |
